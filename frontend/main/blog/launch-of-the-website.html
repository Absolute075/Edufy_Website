Launch of the Website

This day has come.

Today we’re excited to launch the new Edufy website. It brings a cleaner design, faster load times, and a foundation for product updates and student stories.

What’s inside

  • Modern UI with a focus on readability and speed.
  • Blog section for announcements, tips, and community stories.
  • Improved media support: images and inline videos.

Our goals

  • Make exam preparation more transparent and measurable.
  • Share best practices and success stories from our students.
  • Ship features faster and collect feedback earlier.

Roadmap

  • Personal analytics dashboard with progress insights.
  • Speaking mock tests with structured feedback.
  • Resources hub for IELTS preparation.

How you can help

  • Send feedback via email or comments — what’s missing or confusing?
  • Share your study goals — we’ll tailor upcoming posts and features.
  • Report bugs or broken links you notice.

Thank you for being with us from day one. More updates are coming soon.
